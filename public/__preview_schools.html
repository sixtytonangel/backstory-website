Ways to work together

Stories That Matter

45-minute talk · Up to 200 students

An introduction to the what, why and how of telling stories from our lives. I share relatable, personal stories and break them down to reveal a simple storytelling structure. Interactive elements help students start spotting stories in their own lives, and leave them inspired to shape and share stories that matter to them.

Find Your Stories

90-minute workshop · Up to 30 students

Students are led through the process of discovering story ideas from their lives and shaping them into narratives that engage listeners. They work together to help each other find plot and scene details that transport the listener to the centre of the story. At the end of the workshop, each student has a story to tell.

Develop Your Story

90-minute workshop · Up to 30 students

For students to get thoughtful, actionable feedback on their stories from the perspective of listeners. Students come with a loose narrative or skeleton of a story from their life. Working in pairs and groups, they swap stories and learn to listen with intention, guided by me. The group's questions, comments and observations give each storyteller new insight into the strengths and flaws of their story. Students leave equipped to polish it into a cohesive narrative. It's also a wonderful exercise to build trust in a classroom.

Find Your Voice

90-minute workshop · Up to 30 students

Explores the idea of an artist's voice and invites students to notice and develop their own. A moderated discussion on what voice means, then exercises that reveal their voice across different forms of expression: spoken, written, visual. It ends with reflections on their own voice and ways to keep exploring it in their existing projects. A chance to build creative confidence and instincts, especially important in the AI era.

Longer programmes

Multi-session courses that end in a show, like the storytelling workshop series at Malvern College. I also teach storytelling and creative writing in ECA programmes with PressStart Academy.

Student Story Slam

An annual inter-school storytelling competition I organise with Hong Kong Stories. In 2026, 96 students from 30 schools took part, with the final round at the Fringe Club.
